## Abstract

This synthetic report describes a controlled growth trial of moss protonema
under three light spectra. All values below are invented for demonstration.

## Growth conditions

Cultures were maintained at 22 °C and 65 % relative humidity for 21 days.
Illumination was provided at a photosynthetic photon flux density of
120 µmol m-2 s-1 with a 16 h photoperiod and a 8 h dark period.

The red-dominant spectrum was applied only when the culture density exceeded
0.8 g L-1; cultures below that density received a neutral spectrum.

## Biomass results

Dry biomass reached 1.42 g per vessel under the red-dominant spectrum.
Dry biomass under the red-dominant spectrum was higher than under the
blue-dominant spectrum, which reached 0.98 g per vessel.
The red-dominant condition produced 45 % more biomass than the blue-dominant
condition across six replicate vessels.

Chlorophyll a content was 2.7 mg g-1 in the red-dominant group and 1.85 mg g-1
in the blue-dominant group.

No data on root elongation are available, because protonema does not form roots.

## Definitions

photosynthetically active radiation incident on a surface, expressed in
