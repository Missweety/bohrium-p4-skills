## Abstract

Recovery was 92% in the control group and 71% in the cooled group (difference 21 percentage points, n = 24 per group). The result is an association, not proof of causation.

## Results

The 95% confidence interval for the difference was 8 to 34 percentage points.

## Limitations

Only one cell line was tested, and the sample size was small. The study cannot establish causality or rule out batch effects.
