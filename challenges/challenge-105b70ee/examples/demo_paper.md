# Synthetic trial report (not a real paper)

## Abstract

We enrolled 240 participants with elevated LDL cholesterol in a randomized trial. The intervention group received a daily dose of 20 mg of the test compound, while the control group received placebo.

## Methods

Participants were followed for 3 years after randomization in the outpatient clinic. LDL cholesterol was measured at baseline and at the end of follow-up.

## Results

The mean LDL cholesterol reduction was 1.42 mmol/L (95% CI 1.10-1.74) compared with placebo. The effect remained significant after adjustment for baseline values.

## Discussion

The authors conclude that the intervention was effective in lowering LDL cholesterol over 3 years, although long-term safety data are limited.
