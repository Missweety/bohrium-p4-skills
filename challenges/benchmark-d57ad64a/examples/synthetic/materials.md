## Background

Urban heat exposure is unevenly distributed across neighbourhoods. This
synthetic trial evaluates whether additional street-tree canopy reduces
afternoon surface temperature in a mid-latitude residential district.

## Methods

Surface temperature was logged at 48 stations during July. Each station was
classified as low-canopy (below 15 % cover) or high-canopy (above 35 % cover).
The canopy intervention was applied only when the street width exceeded 12 m;
narrower streets kept their original layout.

## Results

Afternoon surface temperature was 3.2 °C lower at high-canopy stations than at
low-canopy stations. The difference was 4.1 °C on days above 33 °C.

## Limitations

No data on night-time temperature are available, because loggers were powered
down after 20:00 local time. The sample covers one summer season.
