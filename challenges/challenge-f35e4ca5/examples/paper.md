# Dose response study

Figure 1. Cell viability after compound X treatment. Cells were treated for 48 h. Data are mean ± s.e.m. (n = 6). Significance was assessed by two-sided t-test; * p < 0.05; n.s. not significant; ** p < 0.01.

The x axis shows dose in µM on a logarithmic scale; the y axis shows viability in %.
